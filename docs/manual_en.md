# darkTunes Music Group - User Manual

## Welcome

Welcome to the darkTunes Music Group platform! This manual will guide you through the features available on our platform.

## Features

### Public Website

- **Artists:** Browse our roster of artists, view their profiles, and listen to their releases.
- **Releases:** Explore the latest releases from our artists.
- **News:** Stay up-to-date with the latest news and announcements.
- **Videos:** Watch music videos and other content from our artists.
- **Concerts:** Find upcoming concerts and events.

### Artist Portal

If you are an artist with darkTunes, you can access the Artist Portal to manage your profile and view analytics.

- **Profile Management:** Update your bio, genres, and profile photo.
- **Releases & Videos:** Submit new releases and videos for approval.
- **Analytics:** View streaming statistics and sales statements.
- **Invoices:** Create and manage invoices.
- **Messaging:** Communicate with the label directly through the portal.

### Press & Promo Pool

For journalists and media professionals, we offer a dedicated Press & Promo Pool.

- **Press Kit:** Download high-resolution press photos and artist bios.
- **Promo Pool:** Apply for access to exclusive promotional tracks and upcoming releases.
- **Interview Requests:** Submit requests for artist interviews.

## Support

If you need further assistance, please contact us at contact@darktunes.com.
